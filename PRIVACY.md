# Privacy Policy for Unfolks

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Unfolks is a browser extension that shows which Instagram accounts you follow that do not follow you back, and lets you unfollow them.

## What Data We Collect

Unfolks reads information that Instagram already shows you while you are signed in:

- Your Instagram name, username, and profile photo URL
- Accounts you follow, including names, usernames, photos, and whether they follow you back
- Scan time, sort/filter choices, and a temporary pause if Instagram blocks unfollows

Unfolks does not collect your Instagram password. It uses the login session already in your Instagram tab.

## How Data Is Stored

Scan results, profile info, and preferences stay on your computer in the extension’s local storage. A short-lived copy of the latest account or scan update may be kept until you close the browser so the side panel can reconnect if Chrome restarts background work.

This data is not synced to other devices through the extension.

## How Data Is Used

- Build and display the list of accounts that do not follow you back
- Show your name and photo in the panel
- Unfollow accounts you choose
- Space out unfollows and show a wait timer if Instagram temporarily blocks the action

## Third-Party Services

If the extension crashes, an error report may be sent to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) so the problem can be fixed. A report can include the error message, browser version, and extension version. It is not used for advertising.

Sentry’s privacy policy: https://sentry.io/privacy/

Instagram is operated by Meta. Unfolks is not affiliated with Meta. Instagram’s terms and privacy policy apply to your Instagram account.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share it for advertising. The only off-device transmission is optional crash reporting to Sentry, as described above.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Local scan data stays until you remove it (for example by clearing the extension’s data in the browser or uninstalling Unfolks). Crash reports kept by Sentry follow Sentry’s retention settings.

To delete local data: right-click the extension → Remove, or clear site/extension data in Chrome settings.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and this file in the Unfolks repository. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
